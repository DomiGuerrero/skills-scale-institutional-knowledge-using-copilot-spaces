## OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A concise entry point to OctoAcme's project management processes and guidance.

### Project management processes at a glance

OctoAcme manages projects through an iterative lifecycle: initiate work by validating the business need, success measures, stakeholders, and feasibility; plan scope, backlog, responsibilities, dependencies, and milestones; execute in small increments with regular team check-ins, reviews, quality checks, and progress tracking; release with deployment verification and rollback readiness; then capture learnings in retrospectives and track improvement actions. Project Managers coordinate delivery, risks, schedules, and communication, while Product Managers prioritize customer and business outcomes. Teams maintain shared artifacts such as a project charter, backlog, release plan, and risk register, communicate regularly, and use evidence and feedback to improve.
